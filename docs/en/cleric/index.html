Overview

In AION 2 dungeons the Cleric is a healer that has to deal damage: rewards depend on your contribution, and a Cleric who only heals can walk out with nothing. Your party has plenty of dodges and invulnerabilities of its own — top people up after hits, and put everything else into damage.

The build in one sentence

Earth Punishment makes Condemnation always crit, and a crit resets Condemnation — so it fires with no cooldown, while Divine Aura, Bolt and Noble Aura add damage on top.

This mode follows Kaeria's Cleric PvE video guide (Russian, recorded on the Taiwan client on September 28; she says it applies to global too), checked against the video frames on September 30. Skill picks are reversible for free — only arcana cost resources.

Patch Season 1. The build is checked against guides from the global servers: 4 stigmas, cap 20.

Global Season 1: what changes

Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). What that means for the Cleric:

  • 4 stigma slots, capped at 20. Kaeria's Taiwan bar has more; the four in this guide are her picks for global. The level-25 tiers (such as Double Chance on Light of Protection) don't exist here.
  • You are paid for damage. Dungeon rewards depend on your contribution, so a Cleric who only heals can leave with nothing — this whole build is about dealing damage while healing on the side.
  • Bigger groups (5-player expeditions, 10-player sanctuaries): with a Chanter in the party your mantra doesn't stack with theirs — swap Light of Protection out.
  • Lower ping than on Taiwan: animations cancel better, so the macro can weave more basic attacks.

Active skills and passives are identical in the global and KR/TW data; stigmas lose their level-25 tier. The source video was recorded on the Taiwan client with English skill names.

Passives

  • Empyrean Lord's Grace — the most important one: Critical Damage, Double Chance and extra damage on every hit. As high as you can.
  • Earth's Grace — used to buff the party, now only you; still worth leveling.
  • Healing Enhancement — heal boost if you heal a lot.
  • Radiant Benediction — heals nearby allies on attacks; take it if you go all-in on damage.
  • Warm Benediction and Immortal Veil are fine to have; Empyrean Lords' Benediction is for PvP.

First days (aLuckyRO): level Radiant Benediction — it heals the whole party every few seconds while they hit — and Healing Enhancement first. The crit passives pay off only once your gear gives Critical Hit; until then you barely crit.

Gear & stats

  • Gear lines: Might (attack and heal), Combat Speed, Damage Boost; Weapon Damage Boost and Precision are strong too. Skip status resists, evasion and defensive lines in PvE.
  • Rings: craft them as soon as you can — crafted rings have one more line for active skills.
  • Stats: Destruction, Wisdom (Double Chance), Illusion (cooldowns), Time (Combat Speed), Freedom (accuracy), plus Death (Critical Hit) — Kaeria's own pick, a Cleric is always short on crit; Might, Precision, Constitution.

Daevanion boards

Daevanion takes your skills from 10 to 14 and adds stats. Each blue node gives +1 level to its skill.

  • Priority: orange Cooldown and Combat Speed nodes first, then Attack and Critical, then the blue skill nodes, then the defensive orange nodes, passives last.
  • Critical Damage Tolerance and Multi-Hit Resist do nothing in PvE — bosses don't crit or multi-hit.
  • Resetting a board is cheap, and you will rebuild it many times.
  • No Ariel on global: there are five boards, and the level-45 one, Azphel, gives only PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance — in PvE finish the first four.

Macro

Kaeria uses only the in-game macro — no third-party software — so she can still press heals between macro steps. The lines below are read from her hotbar in the video. In AION 2 the bottom slot of a line has the highest priority; the cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.

1. Hotbar lines

3Condemnation line
  1. 3 Chain of Torment
  2. 2 Condemnation
  3. 1 Earth Punishment

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

Earth Punishment goes first so that Condemnation always crits and resets; Chain of Torment keeps the mark that Condemnation needs. Keep Light of Protection out of the line: it is a toggle, and every second press would switch it off.

3. Keys

  • HoldThe macro on RMB together with 3 (the Condemnation line) — Condemnation has no cooldown and cuts the animations of everything else.
  • StaggerWhen the boss is staggered, release 3 and hold only the macro, so Lightning Strike Scattershot gets through.
  • By handHealing Light (she has it on 4) and Radiant Recovery (Q) when someone just took a hit, fully charged Bolt (E), Prayer of Amplification on cooldown.

This is her Taiwan setup, made for a high ping. On global, with lower ping, animations cancel better and you can weave a bit more Earth's Retribution.

  • Skills: Condemnation, Divine Aura, Bolt 16 → 20; Judgment Thunder, Lightning Strike Scattershot, Healing Light, Radiant Recovery 16; the rest 12–14.

  • Play: deal damage for contribution; heal only the people who just got hit.

Overview

The Cleric is the healer, but while leveling it plays as a ranged damage dealer: Chain of Torment marks the target, Condemnation punishes it, and the passive Radiant Benediction heals you and the party on every hit. Heal only when you need to.

Leveling in one sentence

Hold Earth's Retribution for mana, spam the Chain line with the in-game macro, and put your skill points into attacks rather than heals.

Patch Season 1. The build is checked against guides from the global servers: 4 stigmas, cap 20.

How to fight while leveling

  • Packs: Chain of Torment (hits up to 4) → Condemnation → Divine Aura with the AoE pick → Judgment Thunder.
  • Elites: Debilitating Mark (lower Defense) → Chain of Torment → Bolt charged → Condemnation.
  • Mana: target mode, and weave Earth's Retribution — it restores MP on every cast.
  • Low HP: Healing Light, and Radiant Benediction keeps ticking as long as you hit.
  • Staggered bosses: Lightning Strike Scattershot only works while the target is staggered.
