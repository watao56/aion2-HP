    Ranged DPS · kiter · Bow

    20-metre burst built on buffs, charged Deadshots and bleeds — and the best zoning tools in PvP.

    PvELeveling Medium

    Templar

    Tank · party buffer · Sword & shield

    The main tank: blocks buff the whole party, Judgment turns every shield skill into damage, and more crowd control than anyone in PvP.

    PvEPvPLeveling Medium
  • Updated Oct 3, 2026

    Assassin

    Melee DPS · burst · Daggers

    Insignia stacks, crits that fire Heart Gore and a clone that removes its cooldown — the highest burst in melee, if you stay behind the target.

    PvELeveling Hard
  • Updated Oct 3, 2026

    Sorcerer

    Ranged magic DPS · Spellbook

    A glass-cannon caster that mixes fire and ice: Fire Mark and Blaze for damage, Frost and roots for control, and huge burst behind Element Enhancement.

    PvELeveling Medium
  • Updated Oct 3, 2026

    Elementalist

    Summoner · magic DPS · Orb

    The only summoner: spirits of fire, water, earth and wind fight for you, every spirit skill charges Elemental Fusion, and curses tick on the target.

    PvEPvPLeveling Hard
  • Updated Oct 3, 2026

    Cleric

    Healer · earth and lightning damage · Mace

    The healer who levels by hitting: Chain of Torment marks the target, Condemnation punishes it, and every hit heals the party.

    PvELeveling Medium
  • Updated Oct 3, 2026

    Chanter

    Melee support · buffer · Staff

    A staff fighter who buffs the whole party: mantras for damage and speed, Power of the Storm for cooldowns, and a Dark Crush combo that still hits hard.

    PvELeveling Medium
